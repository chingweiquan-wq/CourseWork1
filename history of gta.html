History of Grand Theft Auto V

From development ambitions to becoming one of the most successful entertainment products in history.


1. Development and Announcement (2008-2011)

Following the massive success of *Grand Theft Auto IV*, Rockstar North began conceptualizing GTA V around 2008. The developers aimed to reinvent the core gameplay structure by shifting away from a single protagonist and introducing a multi-character narrative. The game was officially announced in November 2011 with its debut teaser trailer.


2. Record-Breaking Launch (2013)

Released on September 17, 2013, for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360, GTA V shattered industry records by grossing $800 million on its first day and $1 billion within three days. Players praised its satirical take on modern Southern California, expansive open world, and the innovative ability to switch between three distinct protagonists: Michael, Franklin, and Trevor.


3. GTA Online and Multi-Generation Expansion (2013-Present)

Shortly after single-player launched, *GTA Online* debuted, transforming the game into a continuously evolving multiplayer sandbox. Over the years, Rockstar ported the game across multiple console generations (PS4, Xbox One, PC, and later PS5 and Xbox Series X/S), ensuring it remained a permanent fixture in global gaming culture.
