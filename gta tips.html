Top 3 GTA V Tips and Tricks

Essential secrets to survive Los Santos, make cash, and master missions.

#1. Use Franklin's Special Ability in High-Speed Chases

Franklin's special skill allows him to slow down time while driving vehicles. This is an absolute lifesaver during tight turns, chaotic traffic dodges, and sharp escapes from the Los Santos police department.



#2. Master Stock Market Investments with Lester's Assassination Missions

To make massive amounts of money in story mode, save most of Lester's assassination missions until after you complete the main story. Invest all your characters' cash into the targeted competitor stock on the BAWSAQ or LCN markets before starting the mission, then cash out after stock prices skyrocket!


#3. Quickly Lose Cops by Hiding Out of Sight

When you have a wanted level, escaping the police's direct line of sight isn't enough—you have to leave their flashing radar cone area. Ducking into underground train tunnels, multi-story parking garages, or alleyways out of their vision is the fastest way to drop your stars.
