Top 3 Red Dead Redemption Tips and Tricks

Essential survival skills to master Dead Eye, bond with your horse, and stay alive in the frontier.

#1. Master Dead Eye Targeting and Manual Tagging

As you progress through Arthur Morgan's journey, your Dead Eye ability automatically and manually upgrades through different tiers, eventually allowing you to place red X tags manually instead of firing automatically. This shift is crucial for advanced combat encounters. Use manual tagging to target enemies' hands or firearms to disarm them without taking lethal force, or paint multiple headshots across several targets during chaotic ambushes to wipe out rival O'Driscoll or Lemoyne Raider gangs effortlessly before they even have time to return fire.

#2. Keep Your Horse Bonded, Fed, and Equipped

Your horse is far more than just a simple mode of transportation across the massive, unforgiving open world-it is your ultimate lifeline. Make it a regular habit to feed, brush, and soothe your horse to rapidly increase your bonding level from Level 1 all the way to Level 4. Higher bond levels unlock advanced handling maneuvers like sharp sliding stops, lateral steps, and rearing, while also expanding your horse's overall health and stamina pools so it can easily sprint across rugged mountains and escape tight law enforcement pursuits.

#3. Use Bow and Arrow for Pristine Pelts

If your goal is to craft the best trapper outfits, custom camp upgrades, and expanded satchels, you will quickly learn that standard quality animal pelts won't cut it—you need flawless 3-star animal ratings. Shooting large or medium animals with high-powered repeaters or rifles will often damage the hide and ruin the rating. Instead, equip your hunting bow loaded with improved arrows or poison arrows, study the animal first using your binoculars, and aim directly for a clean, silent head or heart shot to secure a pristine pelt every single time.
