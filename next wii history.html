History of Wii Sports

Continuing the legacy of Wii Sports into 2026, the franchise reached a massive milestone with the brand-new entry, Nintendo Switch Sports Resort, released on October 22, 2026, for the successor console.

1. The 2026 Game Drops & Version Updates

  • The Return to Wuhu Island: Nintendo officially brought back the beloved, nostalgic open-world setting of Wii Sports Resort, letting players explore the iconic island with updated modern graphics.

  • The 2026 release features a collection of 12 distinct sports, blending classic fan favorites with new additions-including side modes like 100-pin bowling.

  • Wilderness Bound & Experimental Features (September 15, 2026): Highlighted by the introduction of the Dappled Forest biome (featuring red and orange poplar trees, shelf mushrooms, and red shrubs), alongside atmospheric updates like abandoned camps, straw beds, wool stairs/slabs, and craftable sitting cushions.

  • INext-Gen Motion Controls: Leveraging the updated hardware, the game utilizes advanced Joy-Con 2 motion and control tracking to deliver much sharper 1:1 sports simulation.
