History of GTA V

As of 2026, Grand Theft Auto V has enjoyed a remarkable 13-year lifespan, stretching across three console generations and continuing to rewrite the history books for long-term video game success.

1.The 2024-2025 Era

  • Unprecedented Sales Velocity: The game consistently moves roughly 5 million copies every single quarter, officially pushing past 225 to 230 million copies sold worldwide. It stands firmly as the second best-selling video game in history (trailing only Minecraft).

  • GTA Online's Continued Evolution: Rockstar continued to support GTA Online with massive seasonal updates. Major expansions like Agents of Sabotage (late 2024), Money Fronts (mid-2025), and A Safehouse in the Hills (late 2025) kept millions of active players engaged, bringing in hundreds of millions annually through recurring microtransactions and the GTA+ subscription service.

2.GTA V in 2026: The Milestone Year

  • Title Update 1.73 (Mid-2026): Rockstar pushed out major technical and content refreshes, including the Kortz Center Heist update alongside deep upgrades to the in-game Mission Creator tool (giving creators advanced custom actor logic, new cutscene controls, and dynamic props).

  • The Shadow of GTA VI: With the long-awaited arrival of Grand Theft Auto VI arriving on the horizon for late 2026, GTA V's historical narrative has shifted. It is celebrated not just as a standalone masterpiece, but as the longest-running live-service anchor in gaming history-having single-handedly funded and paved the way for Rockstar's next generation.
