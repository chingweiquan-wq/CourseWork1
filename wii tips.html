Top 3 Wii Sports Tips and Tricks

Essential pro strategies to beat Matt in boxing, bowl strikes every time, and crush your friends.

#1. The Ultimate Bowling Strike Trick (Position 3)

To get a strike almost every time in Wii Sports Bowling, move your Mii two steps to the right (or left depending on your spin), turn your throw slightly outward, and release the ball with a clean spin motion right as your arm swings forward. Mastering this alignment makes getting a 300 game effortless!

#2. Master Counter-Attacks in Boxing

In Wii Sports Boxing (especially against the legendary champion Matt), aggressively spamming punches will leave your guard open and tire your character out. Instead, dodge or block your opponent's hook, wait for them to stumble, and counter with rapid straight jabs for a quick knockout.

#3. Use the Swing Delay in Golf for Perfect Power

In golf, rushing your backswing often causes you to overshoot or slice the ball into the rough. Pull the Wiimote back smoothly and pause just for a fraction of a second at the top of your swing before driving forward to ensure 100% accuracy and distance down the fairway.
