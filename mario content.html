Top 3 Mario Kart 8 Content Creators

The elite racers, world record holders, and community legends dominating the track.

#1. Shortcat

Shortcat is widely regarded as one of the most informative and skilled Mario Kart 8 Deluxe creators on YouTube. Famous for his deep-dive analytical videos, item strategy guides, world record time trial tutorials, and high-level online matchmaking, his content helps players of all skill levels drastically improve their race craft.

#2. TWD98 (The Wii Dude)

While known across Nintendo's broader catalog, TWD98 produces some of the most entertaining Mario Kart 8 Deluxe custom track videos, online tournament battles, and DLC wave playthroughs on YouTube. His upbeat commentary and community-driven event videos make him a fan favorite.

#3. Mankalor

Mankalor is a staple creator for Mario Kart news, meta breakdowns, tier lists, and hilarious online matchmaking highlights. His engaging editing style and thorough coverage of game updates and community trends keep racers fully dialed into the competitive scene.
